    Help (H) · Match share
    
      
    
        
    Dev panel help (H or ` to close)
    
        
    The channel always shows "now", and the big moments come once a week. These tools let you visit any moment as if it were live.
    

        
    URL options (address bar)
    
        
    
          
    ?at=
    Start at this moment, then run on normally. The jump links write it for you.
    
          
    ?speed=
    Fast-forward. 60 = a match in 16 s, 10 = gentler. Good for pacing and flow. Watch movement and captions at normal speed.
    
          
    ?seed=
    A different league (any word). Checks whether something is general or a quirk of one league. Same seed, same league, for both of us. Seed f has a shootout.
    
          
    Combine
    ?seed=f&speed=10&at=… A typo stops the page with an error.
    
        
    

        
    Status lines
    
        
    
          
    now
    League time (pasteable as ?at=), speed, FPS. Well under 60 fps means something is slow.
    
          
    season
    Season, regular or playoffs, matches kicked off out of 59.
    
          
    segment
    What the programme is showing and when it ends. "Why is this on now?" Around each match: pre_match, walk_out, live, handshakes, then the post-match show (full_time, highlights, studio, marked "post-match show").
    
          
    on air
    The match being drawn: score, time in, live or highlights (which clip), your pick or the channel's (final round).
    
          
    next
    The next kick-off.
    
          
    seed
    Which league, build time, events stored.
    
        
    

        
    Jump links (keep your seed and speed)
    
        
    
          
    This moment
    A bookmark for right now. Right-click, copy, and send it to Claude to show exactly what you saw.
    
          
    Match share
    Click to work out how much of this season's week shows a match being played, live or re-aired (about a second). The aim is well over half; about two-thirds is expected.
    
          
    Last handshakes, Last post-match show
    15 s into the handshakes or the post-match show of the match that finished most recently.
    
          
    ?hl=1.5
    Plays highlights at another speed on this screen only (the setting is highlights.speed).
    
          
    S, T, G, W
    The side panel's tabs, on this screen only: STATS (the match on screen), TABLE (T), TOP (scorers, G), GAMES (W). Clicks work too; every load starts on STATS.
    
          
    Next walk-out
    15 s into the walk-out before the next match.
    
          
    Next match
    2 min into the next live match.
    
          
    Opening match
    The season's first match: empty table, no form yet.
    
          
    Final day card
    3 min before the final round: the four fixtures and the races.
    
          
    Final round
    6 min into the four simultaneous matches. Test flipping (1–4, 0 = auto) and goal alerts.
    
          
    Semis, final
    2 min into each playoff match: knockout scorebug, overtime, shootouts.
    
          
    Champions
    The champions card. May pause briefly first to work out when the final ends.
    
          
    Off-season
    Retirements, draft lottery, draft.
    
          
    Countdown
    Just before next season: the countdown card and scorebug.
    
          
    Next opener
    Checks the week loops into the next season.
    
          
    Dimmed
    Already passed. Still works.
    
        
    

        
    Keys
    
        
    
          
    `
    Dev panel on/off.
    
          
    H
    This help (while the panel is open).
    
          
    Next studio desk
    (Dev panel jump) Searches ahead for the studio desk (Task 58), which only comes round now and then in the gaps.
    
          
    1–4, 0
    Final round: watch match N; 0 returns to the channel's pick.
    
          
    B
    Layout: Full (the side panel, ticker and buttons) or Broadcast (stands all round, the pitch in the middle, like TV). Every load starts in Full.
    
          
    M
    Sound on/off. The speaker beside the scorebug opens switches for sound, the crowd and the voices. Every load starts silent.
    
          
    V
    The TV guide: today on the channel (also the first button under the side panel). Esc or ✕ closes it. Full only.
