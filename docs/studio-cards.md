## What stands out

1. **The early season is thin.** In the first rounds TOP SCORERS, ASSISTS, STOPS and KEEPERS have a match or two behind them (or say "No goals yet"), the FORM GUIDE is one or two results long, and THE PLAYOFF RACE isn't on yet, so TEAM SPOTLIGHT shows twice a rotation (63 of 343 minutes).
2. **The playoffs keep regular-season cards that have gone stale:** the FORM GUIDE (the regular season's last five), TOP ASSISTS / STOPS / KEEPERS and TOP SCORERS (the regular season's), CAREER's ONE TO WATCH.
3. **After the final** the 7-card rotation shows each card about 3 minutes in all, and beyond HONOURS (the award winners, the No. 1 pick) the draft and the farewells aren't on a card (they're in the off-season show only).
4. **Nothing in the rotation looks back further than this season** except CAREER: no past champions, no records, no history, though the chronicle holds them (the CHRONICLE pane has them).
5. **Money only after the final:** PAYROLLS and BIGGEST SIGNINGS never show in the season.

## Tom's decisions (2026-10-19)

- **The eleven new cards:** all taken, where the table above puts them.
- **The order: a shuffled deck per phase** (early season, mid to late season, final round to the playoffs, the playoffs, after the final), dealt four to a block. No card again until the phase's whole pool has had a turn; a new shuffle never starts with the card that ended the last. Seeded by the league and the moment, so every screen agrees and no day runs in the same order. (Until now: one fixed list per phase, each block carrying on from where the last stopped.)
- **Empty cards skipped:** a card with nothing to say (no goals yet, no record within reach) is passed over, not shown thin.
- **Fixed slots stay:** the promo leads its block in the big-match gaps, and **LATEST RESULTS is the first card of the block straight after a match**; everything else is dealt from the deck.

**Next:** the pools per phase drafted from the table, then the new cards' mock-ups, then the deck and the new cards built together.
