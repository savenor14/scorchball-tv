Clips: the vertical camera

The real channel in a tall frame, following the ball, for Shorts and TikTok (getting the word out, docs/launch-plan.md). Record it with OBS (a Window Capture of this browser, cropped to the tall box, with the desktop's sound). npm.cmd run clip -- --list lists the moments worth a clip (refresh after); pick one and LOAD (it starts 20 s early; the click turns the sound on). Keys (they work after clicking the picture too, and never reach the channel's own): arrows nudge the framing, K keeps the camera still (the arrows move it), F back to following the ball, X hides these controls (and shows them again).
